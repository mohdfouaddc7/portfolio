The machine

Five parts. One operating system.

Beyond the marketing, an entire operating system was engineered into the business itself: the machinery that carries a customer from first text to paid repair, and tells the team what needs attention along the way.

01

The field app runs the jobs

Customers, quotes, invoices, payments, and the technicians' schedule all live in one place. It is the system of record for the work itself.

02

The CRM talks to the customer

Every text and email goes out from here. Calls are answered by Kathy, the AI receptionist, who screens, routes, and takes details when the team is busy. Each customer is tracked on one pipeline, from new lead to paid repair.

03

A server ties them together

When something happens in the field app (a quote goes out, an invoice is paid, a visit is booked), a custom-built server moves the customer forward in the CRM and sends the right text automatically. A connector layer covers the few apps that can't talk to each other directly.

04

Seventy bots keep watch

About 70 bots run on that server all day. They follow up on quotes, catch missed calls, flag mistakes, check payroll, and track ad spend against paying customers, so the humans only step in where judgment is needed.

05

The team dashboard shows what’s next

One page. What needs attention, and who owns it. The reason nothing falls through the cracks.

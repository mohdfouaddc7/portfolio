Case Study · Local Service

Mr. Pool Leak Repair

Local service, rebuilt as an operation.

mrpoolleakrepair.com

A systems-first engagement that took Mr. Pool Leak Repair from a service business running on Google Ads alone into a multi-channel, multi-location lead engine, and then built the operating system underneath it: Jobber running the jobs, GoHighLevel talking to every customer, a custom server connecting the two, seventy bots keeping watch, and one dashboard telling the team what to do next.

9Service areas across DFW

The brief

Good business. Missing operation.

Mr. Pool Leak Repair had a real performing business: Google Ads and GMB driving inbound, excellent technicians, loyal repeat customers. What it didn't have yet was a system: multi-channel attribution, operational SOPs, training infrastructure, and a content engine built for both search engines and the AI systems reading them. The opportunity: turn a small service business into a scalable operation.

The work

Six pillars. One operation.

01

Multi-channel lead engine

Strategy, creative, and media buying across Google Ads, Meta, Yelp, and Bing, with ad copy aligned to landing pages and conversion optimization applied end-to-end.

02

Local SEO, nine locations

Nine Google Business Profiles managed across the DFW metroplex, paired with local citation building and NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across top directories, plus the ongoing verification and suspension work most service businesses never get right.

03

Knowledge hub

A full technical audit, then dozens of deeply interlinked guides covering leak detection, cost breakdowns, service-area pages, and DIY-vs-professional calls. A deliberate internal-linking architecture distributes topical authority across the site, giving search engines (and AI models reading them) one coherent source to cite.

04

A custom operating system

Jobber runs the jobs, GoHighLevel talks to the customer, a purpose-built server connects them, and a fleet of bots keeps watch. An entire CRM, engineered for one business. The full breakdown is below.

05

Operations & training

Company-wide systems: SOPs, training programs, and internal knowledge bases for CSR, dispatch, sales, and repair teams, plus active training on call handling and dispatcher workflows.

06

LLM & AI search

The knowledge hub doubled as AI-search infrastructure. Structured content, consistent entity signals across the web, and LLM confusion audits, so ChatGPT, Gemini, and their peers cite Mr. Pool, not a competitor.

The machine

Five parts. One operating system.

Beyond the marketing, an entire CRM was engineered into the business itself: the machinery that carries a customer from first text to paid repair, and tells the team what needs attention along the way.

01

Jobber runs the jobs

Customers, quotes, invoices, payments, and the technicians' schedule all live in one place. It is the system of record for the work itself.

02

GoHighLevel talks to the customer

Every text and email goes out from here. Calls are answered by Kathy, the AI receptionist, who screens, routes, and takes details when the team is busy. Each customer is tracked on one pipeline, from new lead to paid repair.

03

A server ties them together

When something happens in Jobber (a quote goes out, an invoice is paid, a visit is booked), a custom-built server moves the customer forward in GoHighLevel and sends the right text automatically. Zapier covers the few apps that can't talk to each other directly.

04

Seventy bots keep watch

About 70 bots run on that server all day. They follow up on quotes, catch missed calls, flag mistakes, check payroll, and track ad spend against paying customers, so the humans only step in where judgment is needed.

05

Scout shows the team what's next

One page. What needs attention, and who owns it. Scout is the team's dashboard, and the reason nothing falls through the cracks.

In one line: Jobber does the work, GoHighLevel talks to the customer, the server connects them, the bots keep watch, and Scout shows the team what to do next.

Cubbies

A children's brand, launched into market.

cubbieseg.com

A ground-up launch for an Egyptian baby and toddler fashion brand, digital-first by design and multi-channel from the start. Brand system and Shopify storefront built from scratch, marketplace distribution secured on Homzmart, and the paid, social, SEO, payments, shipping, and automation stack that makes a DTC launch actually work from day one.

2,000+Orders shipped in 6 months
2Sales channels: DTC site + Homzmart vendor
60+Five-star customer reviews

The brief

Not a website. A business.

The brief was not to design a storefront. It was to launch a business. Brand, storefront, catalog, payments, shipping, marketing channels, marketplace distribution, customer service, reviews, and the automations tying them together, all standing up at once, in a category where trust with parents is the entire funnel.

The work

Seven pillars. One launch.

01

Brand & identity

Full identity system: logo, color, typography, and tone of voice. Built to feel warm and trustworthy without sliding into generic baby-pink territory. Guidelines tight enough to scale across packaging, social, site, and customer service.

02

Shopify, built from scratch

Custom storefront built on Shopify, mobile-first for Egyptian shoppers. Category architecture, PDPs, cart, and checkout engineered for conversion in a category that runs on impulse and reassurance.

03

Product catalog

Full catalog built from zero: product pages, size guides, care instructions, and the merchandising structure that makes browsing actually work. No placeholder SKUs. A store that reads as an established brand on day one.

04

Payments & shipping

Credit card and Visa payment gateway integrated into the storefront, plus end-to-end shipping company API integration. Orders flow from checkout to doorstep without a person rekeying data between three systems.

05

Acquisition

Launch-day paid media across Google and Meta, SEO foundation with technical setup and content plan, and a social-first launch calendar with creative calibrated for parents, not algorithms alone.

06

Automations & reviews

Email, WhatsApp, and CRM automations connecting every customer touchpoint. Plus a review management program that turns post-purchase happiness into visible social proof on every PDP.

07

Marketplace distribution

Onboarded onto Homzmart as a vendor in their Baby Fashion launch section. A second sales channel beyond the DTC site, putting Cubbies in front of an existing marketplace audience with built-in trust signals from day one.
